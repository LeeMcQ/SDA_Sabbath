Delete your SDA Sabbath account

Last updated 3 November 2026. App: SDA Sabbath, made by Lee McQuire.

An account is optional and only needed for the SDA Sabbath cloud (usage statistics, stories and messages). Everything else in the app works without one.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open SDA Sabbath and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Press Delete my account and confirm.

The deletion happens straight away. SDA Sabbath keeps working on your device afterwards, signed out.

Can’t sign in any more?

Email the Information Officer at [email address to be added] from the email address of the account, with the subject “Delete my SDA Sabbath account”. The account is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, and name if you used Google sign-in).
  • Your messages and the replies, and the stories you sent.
  • The usage statistics of the devices you signed in on.

What is kept

  • Nothing that identifies you. Totals that cannot be linked to you (for example “120 churches used the app in October”) may stay.
  • Backups of the database are overwritten within [backup retention to be confirmed, for example 7 days].
  • Your settings, plans, hymn words and Bibles on your own devices are not in the cloud. Remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser, or with Reset in Settings.
  • Songs you shared that were approved stay in Shared Hymns for every church (without your account); ask the maintainer to remove one.
