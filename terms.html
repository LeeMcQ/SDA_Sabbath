Showing song words or Bible text on a live stream or a recording (for example the OBS lower thirds or YouTube) is not covered by a normal CCLI Church Copyright Licence: your church needs a CCLI Streaming Licence (or the copyright owner’s permission) for copyrighted songs, and must check the Bible translation’s rules for broadcast.

Translation accuracy

Live translation (for example Afrikaans to English captions) is made by a computer and can be wrong, leave things out or change the meaning. It helps people follow; it is not an official or exact translation. Bible verses it recognises are shown from a public-domain English Bible (WEB or KJV), which can read differently from the translation being preached from.

AI features

AI output can be wrong, can be doctrinally wrong, and can be unsuitable. Read and check everything against Scripture before it is shown. AI pictures are marked “AI-generated”. You use your own account and key with the AI provider, and their terms and charges apply to you. Set a spending limit with them.
